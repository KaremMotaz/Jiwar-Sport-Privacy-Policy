# Privacy Policy — Jiwar Sport

**Effective date:** November 12, 2026
**Last updated:** November 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **[COMPANY LEGAL NAME]** ("**we**", "**us**", "**our**") collects, uses, shares and protects your personal data when you use the **Jiwar Sport** mobile application (package `com.jiwarsport.app`) and its related services (together, the "**App**").

## 1. Who the App is for

Jiwar Sport connects sports clubs and academies with their **members, families, players and coaches**. Depending on your account type, you may use features for club membership, family management, sports training, matches, health and recovery tracking, community news, events and club services.

## 2. Data we collect

We only collect data needed to provide the App's features. Some data is optional and only collected when you use a specific feature.

### 2.1 Data you give us

| Category | Examples | When |
|---|---|---|
| **Account & identity** | First and last name, email address, phone number, password (stored only in hashed form on our servers), account type (member, player, coach, etc.), selected club, acceptance of the Terms | Sign-up, login, password reset |
| **Phone verification** | One-time passcodes (OTP) sent to your phone number | Sign-up, login, password reset |
| **Profile** | Full name, city, profile picture, favourite sports, interests, preferences | Settings and profile |
| **Membership** | Membership number and card, QR code, membership history, benefits used, guest passes, invited guests | Membership features |
| **Family members** | Names and details of family members, including **children**, that you add; family schedules; family safety contacts (name, phone number); family card limits | Family features |
| **Health & fitness** *(sensitive)* | Medical information, medical notes and documents, injuries (type, body part, grade, dates), body measurements (height, weight, body fat %), health assessments, readiness, habit logs, daily reports | Player health & recovery features |
| **Sports performance** | Training sessions, attendance, exercises, goals, skills, achievements, certifications, match plans, lineups, match stats, evaluations, coach notes, conduct logs, error logs | Player and coach features |
| **Documents & media** | Identity or verification documents, trust documents, medical documents, sports certifications, training videos, photos | When you upload them |
| **Vehicles & parking** | Vehicle details (e.g. plate number, model), parking entry and exit logs | Parking features |

| **Applications** | Applications to academies, championships and opportunities | When you apply |

### 2.2 Data collected through device permissions

We only ask for these permissions when you use the related feature. You can refuse or withdraw them at any time in your device settings, and the rest of the App will keep working.

| Permission | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Camera** | To scan membership QR codes and, if you choose, to take a photo (for example, a profile picture). |
| **Photos / files** | To choose a profile picture or upload documents and videos that you select. |
| **Precise & approximate location** | **Only when you report a safety incident**, to record where it happened. We do not track your location in the background. |

### 2.3 Data collected automatically

- **Authentication tokens** that keep you signed in. These are stored securely on your device.
- **Basic technical data** our servers receive with every request, such as IP address, device and OS type, App version, and the time and date of the request. We use it to operate, secure and fix the service.

The App **does not** contain advertising, does not use advertising IDs, and does not include third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

### 2.4 Data stored on your device

The App stores some data locally (secure storage, local cache and preferences): sign-in tokens, language and display preferences, and cached content so screens load faster. This data is removed when you log out, delete your account or uninstall the App.

## 3. How we use your data

We use personal data to:

1. **Provide the App:** create and manage your account, verify your phone number, and show your membership, family, training, match, health and club data.
2. **Connect you with your club:** let your club, academy, coaches and staff manage memberships, training, evaluations, events, access and services.
3. **Process transactions:** wallet top-ups, renewals, tickets and orders.
4. **Keep people safe:** handle incident reports, SOS requests and missing-child alerts, and notify your safety contacts and club security.
5. **Personalise features:** for example, AI suggestions, opportunities and insights based on your sports activity and preferences.
6. **Communicate with you:** send OTPs, service messages and notifications according to your notification preferences.
7. **Secure and improve the service:** prevent fraud and abuse, fix errors and keep the App working properly.
8. **Meet legal obligations:** comply with applicable laws and lawful requests from authorities.

We **do not sell** your personal data, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

### Legal basis

We process your data based on: the **contract** between us when you use the App; your **consent**, for optional data, sensitive health data and device permissions; our **legitimate interests** in keeping the service secure and working; and **legal obligations**. Where we rely on consent, you may withdraw it at any time. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that already happened.

## 4. Sensitive data

Health, medical and injury information, identity documents, children's data and location are **sensitive personal data**. We collect them only for the features you use, with your explicit consent (or a parent's or guardian's consent for a child). Access is limited to you and to authorised staff of your club or academy (such as your coach or club medical staff) who need it for that purpose.

## 5. Children's data

- Children (under 18) **do not create their own accounts**. A **parent or legal guardian** adds a child to the App and manages the child's data, and is responsible for doing so.
- The parent or guardian can view, correct or delete the child's data at any time from the App or by contacting us.
- We use children's data only to provide club, sports and safety services (for example, training, attendance and missing-child alerts). We never use it for advertising or profiling for marketing.
- If we learn that we have collected a child's data without a guardian's consent, we will delete it.

## 6. Who we share data with

We share personal data only as described below:

| Recipient | What & why |
|---|---|
| **Your club or academy** | The club or academy you select, and its authorised staff (administrators, coaches, medical and security staff). They receive the membership, family, player, training, health, safety and transaction data needed to provide their services to you. The club is responsible for how its staff use this data. |
| **Payment gateway** | A licensed third-party payment provider processes your card payments. **We do not receive or store your full card number or CVV.** We only receive the transaction result and reference. The payment provider's own privacy policy applies to the data you give it. |
| **Service providers** | Companies that host our servers, deliver SMS/OTP messages or provide similar technical services for us. They work under contract, only on our instructions, and must protect your data. |
| **Your safety contacts** | When you send an SOS or a missing-child alert, the contacts you added and club security receive the information needed to respond. |
| **Authorities** | When the law requires it, or to protect someone's life, safety or rights. |
| **Business transfer** | If our business is merged, acquired or sold, your data may be transferred, and this Policy will continue to protect it. |

Other users only see what you choose to make visible, such as your comments on community news.

## 7. International transfers

Our servers or service providers may be located outside Egypt. If so, we transfer data only in line with Law No. 151 of 2020 and with appropriate safeguards that protect it to a level no lower than this Policy.

## 8. Data retention

We keep personal data **only as long as your account is active** or as long as needed for the purposes in this Policy. Then:

- When you **delete your account**, we delete or anonymise your personal data within **[30] days**.
- Some records (for example, invoices and payment records) may be kept longer when the law requires it, for **[up to X years]**. They are then deleted.
- Backups are overwritten on a regular schedule.

## 9. How we protect your data

- All data between the App and our servers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS).
- Sign-in tokens are stored in the device's secure storage (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain).
- Device backups of App data are disabled.
- Access to personal data is restricted to people who need it, and sensitive data receives extra protection.

No system is 100% secure. If a data breach is likely to put your rights at risk, we will notify you and the competent authority as the law requires.
